# 🎯 11.204 Get Insurance Eligibility definition service

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Modules/Value Added Services (VAS)/Analytical Model/Insurance Program/Insurance Program functionalities/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

The use case presents a service to obtain eligibility definitions related to the externalId. The externalId is presented as insuranceProgramCode-versionNumber
 If the versionNumber part is not provided, the service returns Eligibility Definitions of the currently active version of the Insurance Program.

GET <INSR_ADDRESS>/rest/v1.0/insurance-programs/{externalId}/eligibility-definition
