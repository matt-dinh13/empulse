> **Type**: UseCase

## 📝 Notes

This use case describes processing of EOM billing notification for Deals (Insurance Contracts) where the premium fee is calculated in AM (i.e. with the setting: the First Period Trigger in CONTRACT_SIGNING_DATE_AND_OFFSET, CONTRACT_ACTIVATION_DATE_AND_OFFSET, FIRST_TRANSACTION_SIGN_DATE_AND_OFFSET and the Insurance Period duration set to BILLING_PERIOD).
