# 🎯 08.218 Process Account Closure notifications

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Modules/Contract Services (COS)/Requirement Model/CBL-22680 Service Management Modules for REL/CSI-2978 Process Account Closure notification

## 📝 Notes

The use case presents reading and processing AM notifications about the Account changes (am.topic.account.closure exchange).
On Account Payoff and Writeoff notifications, the functionality based on the setting of services related to account (contract services) perform changes of statuses, possibly operation statuses.
On Account Termination notification, the functionality terminates all active Services related to the Contract (Account)
