# 🎯 13.050 Cancel Contract Supplement

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Modules/Supplements (SUP_NG)/Analytical Model/Contract Supplements/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

The use case presents a method for cancellation of the unprocessed Contract Supplements.
The unprocessed means Contract Supplements which are still not accepted within the processing. In reality, there can only be canceled requests (Contract Supplements) where related Supplement.Supplement Process Setting.Status has Cancel Manually flag allowed.
