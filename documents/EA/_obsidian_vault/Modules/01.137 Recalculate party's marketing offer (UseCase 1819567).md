# 🎯 01.137 Recalculate party's marketing offer

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Modules/Marketing Offer/Analytical Model/Marketing Offers Recalculation/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

The purpose of this technical use case is to initiate the recalculation of marketing offers for particular party by performing the following sub-operations:

	
- Update the data of a respective party stored in CIF (with data collected on a front-end of source application) either in customer or an applicant structures
	
- Provide the additional offer recalculation input data, that are not directly related to the party, to external Party Scoring Tool (PST) module in order to trigger the scoring of the party and subsequent recalculation of its marketing offers itself.
