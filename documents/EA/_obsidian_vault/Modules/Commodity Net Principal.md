# 📋 Commodity Net Principal

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Functional»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Modules/Product Calculator/Analytical Model/Product Calculator Engine/Business Rules/Auxiliary evaluations/Financial calculations

## 📝 Notes

Calculation of Commodity Net Principal (CNP) base type

CNP = (Commodity price/Goods price) * Net principal
- Commodity price - Price of commodity for subvention
- Goods price (GP) - total price of all commodities of offer, see Goods Price (GP) in Base Type 
- Net principal - calculated as Goods price - Down payment, see Net Principal (NP) in Base Type
