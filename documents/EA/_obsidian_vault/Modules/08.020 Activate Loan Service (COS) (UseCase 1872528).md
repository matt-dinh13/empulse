# 🎯 08.020 Activate Loan Service (COS)

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Modules/Contract Services (COS)/Analytical Model/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

The use case presents method for activation of Service related to Contract (incl. Insurance). Activation means "switch-on" the Service either for the first usage, or an activation after previous Service deactivation (switch-off).
Notice: the UC 01.050 Change insurance prolongation is not supported here and stays in the BSL
