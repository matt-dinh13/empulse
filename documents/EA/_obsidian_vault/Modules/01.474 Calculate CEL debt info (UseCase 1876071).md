# 🎯 01.474 Calculate CEL debt info

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Modules/Debt catalogue/Analytical Model/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

This use case is used to recalculate all values CEL or SAI with installments debt in the debt catalogue and updates calculated values in database. 
Update of debt catalogue may be invoked for two main reasons:

	
- Follow-up action of defined events on contract (credit account), which can lead to change of some data in debt catalogue (e.g. pairing / un-pairing of payment from contract, change of installment schedule)
	
- Periodic update at the time of due date of any contract's installment (e.g. some values in debt catalogue will change when due date of installment occurs and client doesn't send his payment)
