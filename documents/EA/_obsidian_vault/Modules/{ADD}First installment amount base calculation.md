> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Functional»

## 📝 Notes

{ADD PCG-5019} 
For country PH 
In product calculator is used following algorithm: 
FIN = A + SUM (MF) + SUM (IFIF)
where
FIN – first installment
A - annity
MF - monthly fees
IFIF - in first installment fee

For monthly fees using Monthly fees amount definition.
For one time in first installment fees using In first installment fees amount definition.
