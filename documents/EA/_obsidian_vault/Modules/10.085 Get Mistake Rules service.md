# 🎯 10.085 Get Mistake Rules service

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Modules/Ticketing (TCK)/Analytical Model/COMMON for Ticketing/Validation rule definitions

## 📝 Notes

A service for getting a list of MistakeRules based on MistakeRuleType and DocumentTypeCode (optionally) parameters. The list is used for creation special types of Tickets (mistake tickets).

e.g. https://tck.in00c1.in.infra/tck/rest/mistakerules?ruleType=CONTRACT_REGISTRATION&documentTypeCode=CLIENT_PHOTO_COMMODITY
