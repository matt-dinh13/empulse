# 🔷 Billing day mapping

> **Type**: Class
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Modules/Product Calculator/Analytical Model/Installment Schedule Dates/REL/Logical Data Model

## 📝 Notes

This entity serves for finding a correct billing day of a contract.
Key is generated by a country-specific algorithm. Keys are mapped to billing days. 
Setting is global for all revolving contracts.

Typically, billing days are determined on basis of due date. In this case, key is equal to days in month.

Example: Billing date of contracts with due day = 20 should be set to 5th day of month. Key will be set to 20, billingDay to 5.
