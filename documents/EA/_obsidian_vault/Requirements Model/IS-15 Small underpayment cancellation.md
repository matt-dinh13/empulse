# 📋 IS-15 Small underpayment cancellation

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Functional»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/IS/IS-15 (CBL-71) Small underpayment cancellation

## 📝 Notes

What:
Implementation of CBL-71
Why:
Currently the small underpayment (or gift payment) which has been already presented on the contract can be canceled in case of later receiving incoming payment. This behavior cannot be accepted in Kazakhstan, because of violation of legal requirements – bank cannot return the payment back if it was presented to the client.

Changes:
Gift payment - is never canceled, no changes needed
Small underpayment will be canceled with payment cancellation.
Small underpayment cannot be canceled after receiving of payment.
