# 📋 CBL-415 Set maximum length of insurance service

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Epic»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/CLM/CLM-339 (CBL-415) Set maximum length of insurance services

## 📝 Notes

HCVN requires to have the possibility to set the insurance length period shorter than the lengths of contract.  

	
- Business case

For insurance of mobile phone commodities HCVN requires to have the possibility to set the insurance period shorter than there is the length of contract. The maximum lengths of insurance of contract will be then set as minimum of insurance length set on insurance service or length of contract.

	
- Business story

Currently in HCVN more types of insurance are used (including Life insurance, Motorbike insurance, Mobile phone insurance). For insurance of mobile phones it is required the length of insurance to be 12 month maximum (according to agreed conditions with partners). 
The final length of insurance will be then minimum (days of insurance length, length of contract). 

	
- 
	
- Possibility to specify the maximum length of insurance



Possibility to set the maximum number of days of insurance on insurance service. 

	
- 
	
- Specify the length of insurance for contract



The maximum length of insurance for particular contract shall be set as min(days of insurance length on insurance service, length of contract).
