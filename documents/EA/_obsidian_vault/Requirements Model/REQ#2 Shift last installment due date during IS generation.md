# 📋 REQ#2 Shift last installment due date during IS generation

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Story»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/In process/ISPAY/PAYM-1728 (CBL-4595) Change of the last due date

## 📝 Notes

Based on a new global parameter we will check last installment due date of a contract after each IS generation and in case it is a non-working day we will shift it to the following working day. Update of due date and customer due date of the last installment is needed.
