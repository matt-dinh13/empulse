# 📋 IS-421 (CBL-300) LOXON Additional Credit Card Fields

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Epic»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/IS/IS-421 (CBL-300) LOXON Additional Credit Card Fields

## 📝 Notes

Currently LOXON General and/or Financial Data screen are not yet displaying important credit card information. 
Below are the credit card information that need to be displayed :
1.	Minimum Payment  -> It refer to Minimum Instalment field in AM UI 
2.	Credit Limit -> It refer to Credit Limit field in AM UI.
3.	Cash Limit -> It refer to Cash Limit field in AM UI.
4.	Available Credit Limit -> It refer to Available Balance in AM UI.
5.	Available Cash limit -> It refer to Available Cash Balance in AM UI. 
6.	Credit Card Block Code & Block Reason  
If this is not deployed, the Collections will not be able to service Credit Cards efficiently and not able to provide information on call to debtor on Credit card.
