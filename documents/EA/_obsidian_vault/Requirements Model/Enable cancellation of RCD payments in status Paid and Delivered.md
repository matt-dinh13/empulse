# 📋 Enable cancellation of RCD payments in status Paid and Delivered

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Story»

## 📝 Notes

In order to enable cancellation of Paid/Delivered LOP RCD transactions via DisbursementMessage we need to change the validation rules in UC 05.701. We will enable this feature by turning global parameter CancelPaidRcdEnabled on.
