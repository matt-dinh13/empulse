# 📋 CBL-1855 Full early repayment services changes

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Epic»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/IS/IS-393 (CBL-1855) Full early repayment services changes

## 📝 Notes

In order to be align with Strategy 2020, following changes of FER services for CEL are required from KZ:

	
- Add possibility to use FER even during moratorium, but with different calculation of FER amount (e.g. including interest in future installments, when customers need to close their contracts, but the requirements for FER/COP are not met)
	
- Add possibility to create FER which will include GIFT payments for calculation of FER amount in order to increase level of customer’s loyalty, to keep competitiveness in the unsecured lending market, to launch of the “Financial protection” project
