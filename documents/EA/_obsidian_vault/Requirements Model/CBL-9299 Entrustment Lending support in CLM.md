# 📋 CBL-9299 Entrustment Lending support in CLM

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Story»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/CLM/CBL-9299 (CLM-2895) Entrustment Lending support in CLM

## 📝 Notes

To allow the entrustment lending in VN (full financing of the contract by a third party partner, where HCI serves only as an originator and an administrator of loans), it is needed to do some system modifications:

	
- Configure Contract Party Structure and Contract Ownership
	
- Set Contract Ownership (with HCVN or LENDER as owner)
	
- Exclude contracts owned by lenders from Debt sale and Securitization


Feature is controlled by CLM Feature Flag useEntrustmentLending.
