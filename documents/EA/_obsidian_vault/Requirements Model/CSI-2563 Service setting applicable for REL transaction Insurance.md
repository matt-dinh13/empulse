# 📋 CSI-2563 Service setting applicable for REL transaction Insurance

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Functional»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/CSI/CBL-19794 (CSI-2470) Additional attributes for Transaction Suplement

## 📝 Notes

Goal of the requirement is enabling Service definition setting also for REL contract with a transaction.
Accepted setting:

	
- Insurance Cancellation Offset


	
- Insurance Termination Offset


These setting parameters are checked on enabling following actions (use cases):

	
- 11.111 Cancel insurance contract manually


	
- 08.451 Get Contract insurance Cancel preview service
	
- 08.452 Cancel contract insurance service


	
- 11.121 Terminate insurance contract manually
	
- 08.453 Get Contract insurance Terminate preview service
	
- 08.454 Terminate contract insurance service
