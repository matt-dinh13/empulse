# 📋 CLM-93

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «epic»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/CLM/CLM-93 (CBL-29) Consolidation (Top-up) for cash loans

## 📝 Notes

Business Objectives: see the original CBL-29 

For the current customers, a consolidation of their running loans will be offered. For example: client has two running loans and we offer him/he cover (foreclose) the current loan debt by using the new loan (contract), and possibly increase of the total loan amount so that client receives extra money. Client will continue to pay new contract with the same due date. The old loans (contracts) are repaid and finished.
