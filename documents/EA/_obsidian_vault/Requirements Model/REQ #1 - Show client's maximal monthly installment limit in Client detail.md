# 📋 REQ #1 - Show client's maximal monthly installment limit in Client detail

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Functional»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/CLM/CBL-3261 (CLM-1298) Available EMI Card offer displayed in Client detail

## 📝 Notes

The maximal monthly installment limit (MMIL) is calculated by the 360 component for each client registered in the system based on some business events generated various systems.
The goal of this requirement is showing MMIL in the Client detail screen. This field will be showed for all countries. The MMIL value is obtained from the 360 component by calling defined service.
