# 📋 CSI-2468 Replace Insurance Service on Contract - DEV

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Functional»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/CSI/CBL-19520 (CSI-2290) Apply feature [Replace service] in Bulk assignment for Payment Service

## 📝 Notes

The Replace Insurance Service on Contract function will be available via web service (REST API) only (i.e. UC 01.782 Replace Contract Service).
Changed and a new Insurance Service must be the same type, and the same setting of following parameters:

	
- First period trigger
	
- First period duration
	
- Next period duration

The Insurance Service change doesn't affect/change the related Insurance Contract from business point of view
