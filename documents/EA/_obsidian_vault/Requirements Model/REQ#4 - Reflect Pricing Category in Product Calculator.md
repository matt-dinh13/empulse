# 📋 REQ#4 - Reflect Pricing Category in Product Calculator

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Functional»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/PCG/PCG-463 RBP online for cash loans (CBL-585)

## 📝 Notes

If Marketing Offer (MO) is selected on input of Product Calculator and Pricing Category is defined in the MO then only such Product Variants (PV) are selected for product offer calculation where PV.PricingCategoryFrom <= MO.PricingCategory <= PV.PricingCategoryTo.
