# 📋 IS-451 Extending AccountBalanceChangeDto for communication purposes between CaBus and LCS

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Story»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/IS/IS-421 (CBL-300) LOXON Additional Credit Card Fields

## 📝 Notes

As LCS (LOXON Collection System), I want to receive following information about credit cards on account balance change: minimum payment, credit limit, cash limit, available credit limit, available cash limit, credit card block type code, credit card block reason code.

As CaBus, I want to be able to send following information about credit cards on account balance change: minimum payment, credit limit, cash limit, available credit limit, available cash limit, credit card block type code, credit card block reason code.
