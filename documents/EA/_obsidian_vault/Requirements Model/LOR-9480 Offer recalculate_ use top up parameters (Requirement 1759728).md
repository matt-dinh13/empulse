## 📝 Notes

As a system, I will be able to recalculate TopUp offer as any other.

Proposed solution - when recalculating offer (e.g. insurance change), use top up parameters from the original offer for calculating the new offer
 

	
- contract number - offer service parameters, just copy to new offer
	
- overlap period - send to calculator and also store to new offer
	
- preferred due data - send to calculator and store to new offer
