# 📋 REQ #4 - Limitation of the mandatory insurance in offering and operation status change

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Functional»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/CSI/CBL-12831 (CSI-617) Mandatory Life Insurance for REL

## 📝 Notes

Goal of this requirement is to:

	
- exclude the mandatory insurance from offers for insurance adding on a contract. The offers are generated in the UC 08.344 Generate insurance offers for contract which is common solution for insurance offers generation for UI and WS.
	
- disable the change operation status of the mandatory insurance via user interface - see UC 08.072 Change insurance operation status
