# 📋 Access rights to view Customer Photos for Field Collections Team Leaders

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Epic»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/CLM/CLM-496 (CBL-558) Access right to view Customer Photo for Field Collection TLs

## 📝 Notes

Client photo is needed in field collection process because visited clients are often denying their identity. Currently Field Collection Team Leaders (TL) do not have the access rights to view client photo and need to ask AMs for this information. Due to a big number of such requests, they are not always able to cater to them and as a result there are 350-400 cases per month where FC is unable to verify the client‘s identity. This task would reduce the cost associated with field visits as field visitors would not have to repeat the visits of the same clients and would have the proper information from the start gathered from their team leaders.
MUCH is not being fully utilized in Early Field and Late Field collection processes and is in pilot test phase, so we need to take this photo from BSL.
This Team Leader should not have the rights to download any other documents except Photo of Client. This is why we would like to have some new access right (e.g. some extension of UC01_128 or UC01_135) which allows to only view and download Photo of Client and not other documents.
