## 📝 Notes

Element for displaying errors from UC 01.272.  Is hidden by default,  after successful execution of UC0.1272 and after deactivation of bar-code scanning functionality

Two message types are proposed to be displayed in this element.

	
- MSG_DOCUMENT_NOT_RECOGNIZED (says e. g. document could not be recognized.).
	
- MSG_DOCUMENT_NOT_IN_DOCUMENTATION (says e. g. document is not part of this contract documentation.).
