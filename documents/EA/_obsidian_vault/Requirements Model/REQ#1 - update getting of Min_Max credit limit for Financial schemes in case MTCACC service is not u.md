# 📋 REQ#1 - update getting of Min/Max credit limit for Financial schemes in case  MTCACC service is not used

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Functional»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/CLM/CBL-9285 (CLM-2988) Credit limits for Fin.schemes without MTCACC

## 📝 Notes

Originally MTCACC should be used for all products with Financial schemes.
Currently there are many products with Financial schemes but without MTCACC, so MIN/MAX credit limits should be taken from Product variants as for product without Financial schemes in this case.

{MOD}Credit limit change offer validation rules  is updated.
