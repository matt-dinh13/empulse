## 📝 Notes

Goal of the task is support of the Income proof document checking within the registration process.

The Income proof of client is checked by Credit expert within the approval process (LAP).� The�Credit expert sets up result of his/her check and this result is propagated to BSL in the LAP response vector.

LOR sets up the result on the�Income proof document attribute.

Based on the attribute value, the registration process allow/disallow the contract registration. In case the registration cannot be done, registration operator creates the mistake document ticket related to the�Income proof document and assigned to credit experts to next processing.�

After the credit expert checks the income proof is properly collected, set up the result on the�Income proof document and close the mistake ticket so that registration can continue.
