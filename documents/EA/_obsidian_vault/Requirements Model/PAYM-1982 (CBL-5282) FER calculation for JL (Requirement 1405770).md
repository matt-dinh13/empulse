# 📋 PAYM-1982 (CBL-5282) FER calculation for JL

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Epic»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/In process/ISPAY/PAYM-1982 (CBL-5282) FER calculation for JL

## 📝 Notes

HCIN requires full early repayment service to be available from disbursement date on these condition:
* 0 to 15 days – no extra interest to be charged to customer, customer returns back the whole amount, no specific request required - COP service will be used for this case
* 16 days to 6 months – customer will pay the whole principal and interest till requested FER due date plus extra fee will be charged to him as a percentage of outstanding principal, service is available on request
* More than 6 months – customer will pay the whole principal and interest till requested FER due date
