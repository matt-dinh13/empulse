# 📋 REQ#1 - Fully mask card number shown in the Contract detail -Card tab and Card detail form

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Functional»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/CSI/CBL-13178 (CSI-690) Separate UI privilege for showing of Card number

## 📝 Notes

PH has a business process that enables user to activate his/her card via mobile application by entering the last four digit of his PAN. Currently it is visible as part of masked PAN for all users.
Therefore a new access right to show PAN is needed, without the right PAN is fully masked (*** is shown).
