# 📋 PAYM-1973 (CBL-5228) CEL Reward - Disbursement channel changes

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Epic»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/In process/ISPAY/PAYM-1973 (CBL-5228) CEL Reward - Disbursement channel changes

## 📝 Notes

This CBL enables to minimize the risks by:
* Ability of manual entering of account number can lead to incorrect entering or entering of intentionally the account number of the manager himself/herself;
* Inability to view the last 4 digits of the card number can lead to an error in the selection of the required card;

As is:
* When choosing the debit/current account for disbursement of the cashback, the SA has the opportunity to fill this account number in two ways:
  * By pressing the button „Use existing account“
  * By manually entering it into the field „Account number“
* If there are two or more debit cards on client, the system offers a choice of one of the cards without specifying at least the last 4digits of the card number.

To be:
* Disable the ability to enter the account number for disbursement of the cashback manually;
* Display the last 4 digits of the card number when choosing one of two or more debit cards for disbursement of the cashback.
