> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/Finished/CSI/CBL-18500 (CSI-2052) Change flag SERVICE_SWITCH_ALLOWED for ACCSTMT service type/CSI-2222 Change LoanService status on defined Account Closure notifications

## 📝 Notes

The use case presents reading and processing AM notifications about the Account Closure (am.topic.account.closure exchange).
On Account Payoff and Writeoff notifications, the functionality based on the setting of services related to account (contract services) perform changes of statuses, possibly operation statuses.
On Account Termination notification, the functionality terminates all active Services related to the Contract (Account)
