# 📋 CSI-3580 COS - Update of cancellation and termination methods

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Functional»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Requirements Model/In process/CSI/CBL-25266 (CSI-3437) VN New insurance on card product

## 📝 Notes

This requirement change the current beaviour of cancel/terminate contract service when Manual cancellation/termination allowed flags is set to TRUE in the Service.
The COS methods won't evaluate Manual cancellation/termination allowed flags setting so contract service can be cancelled/terminated whenever the other conditions are fulfilled
