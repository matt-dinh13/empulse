# 🎯 {ADD}Cancel outgoing payment

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Data manipulation support/HS3.0 and later/Outgoing payment manipulations

## 📝 Notes

{ADD PAYM-551 CBL-691 /}

This mBean changes a status of an outgoing payment from Order generating (G), Paid (P), Waiting (W) or Unpaid (U) to status Canceled (C). This can be requested in case of early error discovery in disbursement information (wrong data on account, partner etc.).
