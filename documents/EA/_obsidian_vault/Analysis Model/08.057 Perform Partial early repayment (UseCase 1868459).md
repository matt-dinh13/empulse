# 🎯 08.057 Perform Partial early repayment

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Management/Loan Service Processing/Loan Option CEL/Partial Early Repayment/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

This use case describes partial regenerating of a standard installment schedule (IS) for particular contract due to partial early repayment request. This operation can be only performed on contracts:

	
- in statuses after contract signing on basis of the financial parameters of the contract.
	
- having a valid PER request created
	
- which fulfill conditions for PER performing

For PER can be used only PER amount from interval <Minimal Amount to Pay calculated by PER service setting plus PER fee amount; the amount promised by the client>.
Regenerating starts from the nearest future standard installment after the installment having due date = PER Due Date and ends until outstanding principal = 0. Two methods for regenerating can be used: shorter term (i.e. the original monthly payment is retained), or lower monthly payment (i.e. the original term is retained). Algorithm for principal and interest generating from the original IS is used.

First, there are created an extraordinary installment with principal part which decrease outstanding principal by PER amount as well as an extraordinary installment with fee part (if PER fee is defined).
Remaining principal is amortized (divided) in standard installments. At this installments regeneration, if any further installment is less than preset value of minimal installment amount, then remaining principal will be added to the last generated installment. 
Original installments after PER due date which are replaced by the new versions are deactivated.
After re-generating the installment schedule the system generates a system event RegeneratedInstallmentScheduleSE.
