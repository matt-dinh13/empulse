> **Type**: GUIElement · **Stereotype**: «lcheckbox»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Sales Network Management/Salesroom/Products on Salesroom/User Interface

## 📝 Notes

Filter for list of products assigned to salesroom.
If checked then product assignments  are hidden where current date+time is out of interval <ValidFrom, ValidTo> else all assignments are displayed.
Default = true.
