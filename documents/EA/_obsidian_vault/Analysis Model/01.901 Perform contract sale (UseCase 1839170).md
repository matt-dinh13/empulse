# 🎯 01.901 Perform contract sale

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Management/Contract sale/Access rights

## 📝 Notes

This use case describes a finishing of the contract sale process within which technical (artificial) payments are created and the status of the contract is changes as well.
The use case is activated by the ContractSoldRequestedSE system event (generated in UC 01.802 Process sold contracts file)
At first the system finds information about contract sale (ContractSale entity), which were created (through the import or manually) before (within another use case). 
The system then creates up to two payments to the full repayment of the debt on the contract:

	
- 1st incoming payment “Contract Sale” with the amount of the sale price
	
- 2nd incoming payment "Deficit and damages" with the amount of the outstanding debt after subtract the sale price


Finally, the system changes:
- the owner of the contract
- the status of the contract on "Sold" 
and generates a system event ContractIsSoldSE.
