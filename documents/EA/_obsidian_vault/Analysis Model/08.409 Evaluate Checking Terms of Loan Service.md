# 🎯 08.409 Evaluate Checking Terms of Loan Service

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Management/Loan Service Processing/Loan Option CEL/Checking Terms of Loan Service/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

The use case evaluates fulfillment terms of loan on Checking Terms of Loan service. It searches for the CHECKTL Contract Services do not have a service request created (i.e. the Contract Services which have not been evaluated, or confirmed yet) and are to be evaluated on the defined date (i.e. date when terms of loan have to be fulfilled). For these contract, penalty is calculated and charged.
If the Checking Terms of Loan service is set for automatic fulfillment evaluation, UC {ADD}01.407 Evaluate CheckTL fulfillment common is called for that.
The evaluation is run at defined time by automatic job - see Evaluate Checking Terms of Loan service job for next information.
