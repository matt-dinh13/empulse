# 📋 SN synchronization - Sales area

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Algorithm»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Sales Network Management/Synchronization/Business rules

## 📝 Notes

Sales Area is filled in according to setting of GlobalParameter.BSLSalesAreaSource from one of:
- Sales Zone
- Sales Region
- Business Area
- Sales District
Note: Sales Area is not automatically filled from the new records sent from HomeSIS.
