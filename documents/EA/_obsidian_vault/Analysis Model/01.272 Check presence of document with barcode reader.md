# 🎯 01.272 Check presence of document with barcode reader

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Management/Contract registration/AccessRights

## 📝 Notes

This usecase is used to support document barcode scanning to confirm delivery of contract document in contract registration tab. To use this functionality user has to press 'Turn on barcode scanning' button in 'Contract registration' tab. Clicking on button will cause that the color and the text of the button will be changed to indicate that bar-code scanning is active (proposed color is green and label 'Scan document barcode')
