# 🎯 05.201 Pair payments to due instalment

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Payments/Incoming payments/Pairing incomming payments/Use Case Model

## 📝 Notes

{ADD PAYM-1885 CBL-4285}
This technical use case finds all contracts (signed, active, paid off) having at least one active and fully paid prepayment installment and performs recouple of incoming payments paired to prepayment instalment parts to repay due instalments by funds held on prepayments.

The recouple is triggerred by scheduled daily job Process pairing in due date of if the global parameter PairToPrepaymentTillDueDate is enabled.
