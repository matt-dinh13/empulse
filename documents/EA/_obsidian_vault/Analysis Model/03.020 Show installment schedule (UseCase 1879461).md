# 🎯 03.020 Show installment schedule

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Installment Schedule/Installment Schedule/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

This use-case gives possibility to display information about installment schedule of any existing loan contract. All installments are shown together with basic information about each installment and its type and structure (e.g. due date of installment, fee/penalty, amount etc.).
This function only ensures showing CEL installment schedule of the contract.
