## 📝 Notes

Field has two parts. First part is localized name of Business type of loan according to rule Business type of loan. Second part is product variant code (Contract.().Product.ProductVariant.Code). Second part will be in bracket (example "Consumer loan (CD01-01)")

Value is active link to Product detail screen.
