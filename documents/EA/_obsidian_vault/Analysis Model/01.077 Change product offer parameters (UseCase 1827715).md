# 🎯 01.077 Change product offer parameters

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Origination/Manage Product Offer/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

This use case allows changing some parameters of product offer, which have no impact on product offer calculation, but have influence on later use of offer for e.g. generation of installment schedule. 
UC is activated from the screen for product offer detail.
