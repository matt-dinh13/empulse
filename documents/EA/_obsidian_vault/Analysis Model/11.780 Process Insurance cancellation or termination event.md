# 🎯 11.780 Process Insurance cancellation or termination event

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Insurance/Insurance Contract/Replacement of Insurance on running contract/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

This technical use case receives and processes two system events: InsuranceSwitchOffSE, InsuranceContractTerminationSE and InsuranceContractCancelationSE.
Based on the cancelled/terminated insurance contract type, there are performed some insurance post termination actions:

	
- Replacement of the cancelled/terminated a standard insurance with the mandatory insurance (requested by ID local authority). This action is only performed for contracts with products without Non-mandatory service flag set.
