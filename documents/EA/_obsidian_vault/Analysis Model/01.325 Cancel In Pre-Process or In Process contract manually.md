# 📋 01.325 Cancel In Pre-Process or In Process contract manually

> **Type**: Requirement · **Stereotype**: «Access Right»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Management/Contract cancellation/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

Basic right for UC activation
Code=UC01_325

Contracts in following statuses and sub-statuses are accessible:

	
- status 'In Pre-Process' and sub-status 'Draft' or 'Client Draft' or 'Approved with cross-sell limits'


Rule Access control to Contract by Salesroom for contract detail is applied, user can access only to contracts originated logged on Salesroom or no restriction if Contract.Created remotely = true.
