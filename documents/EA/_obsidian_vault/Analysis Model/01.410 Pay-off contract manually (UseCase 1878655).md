# 🎯 01.410 Pay-off contract manually

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Management/Contract pay-off/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

This use case enables to pay-off one contract. Pay-off reason can be entered. 
After successful pay-off of the contract the external collection system is notified if it is deployed in the system of respective country.
Notice: "Pay off" activity means, that HC asks the client to immediate repay of all debts related to his contract.

The payoff functionality can process both CEL and REL types of contracts but there is a difference in the sequence of processing:

	
- CEL contracts: After a check payoff possibility, the termination is performed and this use case returns information about termination (successful or not) back to the collection system by calling TerminationStatusService.
	
- REL contracts: After a check payoff possibility, LRP is called via LoyaltyManagementWS in order to initiate loyalty account termination due to pay off, then Cabus AM is called via AccountManagementWS (v6) in order to close the credit account due to payoff. A response about the result of processing is received and processed by COMA.
