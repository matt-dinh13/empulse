# 🖥️ Product preferences

> **Type**: GUIElement · **Stereotype**: «form»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Origination/Manage Product Offer/User Interface Model/Choose Product Offer

## 📝 Notes

This panel contains fields for specifying client's loan preferences.
Initially, the panel is hidden until a calculation method is not selected (see use case 01.010 Choose product offer). Each method has its own instance of this panel, it means when the method is changed, the panel is reloaded.

Localization code: PRD_ProductCalculatorPreferences
