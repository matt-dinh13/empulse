# 🎯 08.262 Process Fees-back service

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Management/Loan Service Processing/Loan Option CEL/Fees-back/Access Rigths

## 📝 Notes

The use case describes application of the Fees-back service on the processed contract.
Application of the service on a contract causes also generation of overpayment installment into the installment schedule. If the service setting allows automatic refund generation, the respective refunds are generated to the overpayments.
