# 🎯 08.080 Add new available Service on Contract

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Management/Loan Service Processing/COMMON for Loan Services/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

The use case describes functionality for adding the new available REL services on REL contract. As a new REL service may be created during the contract lifecycle the user may check it by clicking 'Add available service' button. New REL service is detected by the system and user may add the service to the current contract.
Selection of available services only limited on services associated with contract product.
Not for adding an insurance service.
