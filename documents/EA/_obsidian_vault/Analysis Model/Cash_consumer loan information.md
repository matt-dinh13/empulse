> **Type**: GUIElement · **Stereotype**: «panel»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Management/Contract detail/Show contract detail/User Interface Model/Tab-Payment channels

## 📝 Notes

This panel is only modeling container and cannot be visible distinguishable in application. This panel groups existing elements: 

	
- Total payment per credit
	
- Annuity
	
- Total monthly installment
	
- Installments without advanced EMI
	
- Number of advanced EMI
	
- Presented interest rate
	
- Fully paid installments/all installments


These elements are visible only for ProductType = CEL.
