# 🎯 01.445 Cancel undisbursed contract

> **Type**: UseCase

Purpose of this use case is to find contracts in status 'Signed' which were not disbursed  to client (Contract.ConditonsAccepted = ‘Rejected’) and cancel them (call for them usecase 01.360 Cancel contract).
