# 🎯 01.382 Perform contract consolidation

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Management/Loan Service Processing/Loan Option CEL/Loan consolidation/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

The use case describes functionality for executing loan consolidation. The function is triggered either by CashDisbursementConfirmedSE system event (in case “Top-up” variant of the consolidation when we wait for client disbursement confirmation), or by finishing of UC 01.380 Prepare Contract for Consolidation (in case no disbursement of client is done).
In this moment, internal payments are created (if amount is greater than zero) for all consolidated contract to repay their outstanding debt.
