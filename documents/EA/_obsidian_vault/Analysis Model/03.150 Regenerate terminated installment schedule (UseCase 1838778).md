# 🎯 03.150 Regenerate terminated installment schedule

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Installment Schedule/Termination (Pay off) installment schedule/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

The use case describes way of regenerating the original terminated installment of the contract for new Termination installment date. There is always supposed that a provided new due date of terminated installment has to be in period current date < new due date < original due date.
The regeneration of the terminated installment schedule is mainly intended for contract write-off when it is needed to shift due date of termination installment to write-off date. After the regeneration, accrued income amount must be recalculated for each Installment Part overdue. 

For an example of Termination installment due date change see Example of regenerating Terminated installment schedule.
