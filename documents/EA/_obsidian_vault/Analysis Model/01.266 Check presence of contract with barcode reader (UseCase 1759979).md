# 🎯 01.266 Check presence of contract with barcode reader

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Origination/Contract package tracking/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

This usecase is used to support barcode scanning to confirm delivery of contract in contract package. To use this functionality user has to press 'Turn on barcode scanning' button (button is activated by by default after opening of modal window) in 'Receive contract package' modal window. Clicking on button will case that color and text of button will be changes to indicate that bar-code scanning is active (proposed color is green and label 'Scan contract barcode')
