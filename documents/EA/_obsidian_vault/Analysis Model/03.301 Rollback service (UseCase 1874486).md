# 🎯 03.301 Rollback service

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Installment Schedule/Service timeline/Access rights

## 📝 Notes

{ADD IS-1347}
This use case allows to rollback evaluated service on the contract and move installment schedule to the last IS  version before service evaluation. Rollback is supported for services Gift payment, Grace period, Full early repayment, Partial early repayment, Payment holiday, Loan restructuring, Cooling-off period, Contract early termination. Supported is also rollback of overcharged interest deduction.
