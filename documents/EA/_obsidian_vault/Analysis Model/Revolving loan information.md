> **Type**: GUIElement · **Stereotype**: «panel»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Management/Contract detail/Show contract detail/User Interface Model/Tab-Payment channels

## 📝 Notes

These elements are visible only for business type Credit card loan (system evaluates business rule Business type of loan to check if field should be visible).

A list of the affected fields:

	
- Credit limit
	
- Minimal installment base
	
- Minimal installment rate
	
- Minimal installment fixed amount


The panel is only container invisible in the screen.
