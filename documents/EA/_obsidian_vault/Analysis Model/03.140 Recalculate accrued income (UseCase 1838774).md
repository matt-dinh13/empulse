# 🎯 03.140 Recalculate accrued income

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Installment Schedule/Termination (Pay off) installment schedule/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

This use case serves to recalculation of accrued incomes which have been calculated at the contract termination (payoff). The recalculation is needed as soon as a change on the credit balance occurred, i.e. pairing/unpairing of incoming payment with deposit date < termination date from/to contract, or removing an installment with due date < termination date from the contract schedule.
