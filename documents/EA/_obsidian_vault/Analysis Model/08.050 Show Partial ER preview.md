# 🎯 08.050 Show Partial ER preview

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Management/Loan Service Processing/Loan Option CEL/Partial Early Repayment/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

This functionality allows user to get information needed to the partial early repayment of loan (extraordinary repayment)  in case a client asks for it.
The PER preview is done over one contract selected by the user. Use can chose a type of PER (i.e. shorter term or lower annuity of the remaining installment schedule) and enter of amount client promise to pay.
During this action, following steps are performed:

	
- determination of suitable date for PER


	
- a check of PER request availability


	
- calculation of all needed amounts for PER

Finally, calculated PER amounts are displayed on the screen and operator can inform the client about result.
