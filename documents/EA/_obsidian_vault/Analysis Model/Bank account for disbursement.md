# 🖥️ Bank account for disbursement

> **Type**: GUIElement · **Stereotype**: «form»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Account management/Cardless transactions support/User Interface Model

## 📝 Notes

This panel presents all needed fields needed for entering bank account data of defined country. Following country specific panels are used for that:

	
- KZ - Create/Update bank account - KZ
	
- PH - Create/Update bank account - PH
	
- VN - Create/Update bank account - VN

Data entered into the fieldsl is not stored into database! Only respective enumerations and validations for that are used.

Localization code: ACC_BankAccountForDisbursement
