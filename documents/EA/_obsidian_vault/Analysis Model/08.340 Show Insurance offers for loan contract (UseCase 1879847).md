# 🎯 08.340 Show Insurance offers for loan contract

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Insurance/Insurance Contract/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

This use case describes how to get available insurance suitable for the client's loan contract based on his/her preference and show it on the form.
This function is available only for insurance based on EOM billing period of the REL contracts and CEL contracts. Goods insurance types are excluded from available insurances.
