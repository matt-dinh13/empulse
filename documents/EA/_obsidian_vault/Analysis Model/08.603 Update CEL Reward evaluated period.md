# 🎯 08.603 Update CEL reward evaluated period

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Management/Loan Service Processing/Loan Option CEL/CEL Rewards/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

This use case describes an update of the CEL reward evaluated period when the contract installment schedule is changed due to other loan service is applied. Generally, it should be services which change amount or due date of the installments and does not finish the related contract.
The use case is run based on a LoanServiceRequestExecutedSE and PartialEarlyRepaymentSE system events 

Input:

	
- Contract
	
- Contract Service of CEL reward type (ContractService)
