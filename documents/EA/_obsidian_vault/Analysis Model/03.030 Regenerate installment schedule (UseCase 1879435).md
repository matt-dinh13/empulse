# 🎯 03.030 Regenerate installment schedule

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Installment Schedule/Installment Schedule/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

This use case describes complete regenerating of a standard installment schedule (IS) for particular contract in statuses after contract signing on basis of the financial parameters of the contract.
If required also the extraordinary repayments are transferred into the new schedule.
Original installment schedule and its parts are canceled and archived.

At the end of the use case the system generates a system event RegeneratedInstallmentScheduleSE.

Note: This UC does not invoke regenerating of other than standard installment schedule, i.e. regenerating of special variants of alternative schedule for JFS, grace period, etc. is not included.
