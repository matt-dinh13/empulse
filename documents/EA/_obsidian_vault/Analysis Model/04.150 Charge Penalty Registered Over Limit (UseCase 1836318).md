# 🎯 04.150 Charge Penalty Registered Over Limit

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Installment Schedule/Fees and Penalties/Penalty Limit/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

This UC is activated when any penalty is canceled and the functionality for Penalties Sum Limit is activated (see global parameter IsPenaltiesSumLimit).
System checks if exist any penalties requested to be charged in the past and not fully charged due to penalty limit overdraft (Penalty Over Limit) and if so then calculates available limit for charging of penalties and charges corresponding part of the oldest registered penalties over limit.
