# 🎯 13.120 Get Contract Supplements of Customer service

> **Type**: UseCase
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Analysis Model/Contract Supplements/Transaction Supplement support/Access Rights

## 📝 Notes

The use case present a web service to getting of ContractSupplements related to Customer and optionally Contract and Supplement type (e.g. Transaction Suplement Requests).
Supported contract statuses for which is ContractSupplement returned are defined in SUPPORTED_CONTRACT_STATUSES_EXT parameter

Example of the call: GET 
<BSL_ADDR>/openapi/v1.0/customers/10120853/contracts/3900004053/supplements/transaction
<BSL_ADDR>/openapi/v1.0/customers/10120853/contracts/supplements/transaction
