# 🖥️ Payment provider bank account

> **Type**: GUIElement · **Stereotype**: «lcheckbox»
> **Package**: N/A

## 📝 Notes

Selection of this option allows disbursement of loan through a partner bank (i.e. clients gets money from payment provider).

Option is displayed only when payment channel type "PBA (Payment provider bank account)" is available.
