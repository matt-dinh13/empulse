# 📁 Presented Interest Rates

> **Type**: Package · **Stereotype**: «functionality»
> **Package**: N/A

## 📝 Notes

Presented Interest Rate is interest rate, which is presented outside the system as the real "effective" interest rate (EIR). 
Management of Presented interest rates (PIR) allows definition of the way, how particular PIR is calculated, saved, displayed, printed, etc.
