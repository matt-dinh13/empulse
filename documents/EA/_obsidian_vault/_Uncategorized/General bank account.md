# 🖥️ General bank account

> **Type**: GUIElement · **Stereotype**: «lcheckbox»
> **Package**: N/A

## 📝 Notes

Selection of this option allows disbursement of loan to any bank account (e.g. client's bank account defined during creation of application form).

Option is displayed only when payment channel type "GBA (General bank account)" is available.
