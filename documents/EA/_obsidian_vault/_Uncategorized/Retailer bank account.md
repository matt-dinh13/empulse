# 🖥️ Retailer bank account

> **Type**: GUIElement · **Stereotype**: «lcheckbox»
> **Package**: N/A

## 📝 Notes

Selection of this option allows disbursement of loan through a standard partner (directly at salesroom).

Option is displayed only when payment channel type "SBA (Retailer bank account)" is available.
