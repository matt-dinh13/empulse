# 📁 Marketing Offer

> **Type**: Package · **Stereotype**: «module»
> **Package**: N/A

## 📝 Notes

Code: MOM
Team: PCG
Product Manager: Eduard Sovák
Analyst: Eduard Sovák

Description:

	
- Storage of product Marketing Offers (MO) in internal BSL structures. MO is set of limits used as input for Product Offer calculation.
In case of SAS implementation keeps only Marketing Offers already used for Product Offer calculation (set of client's MO is retrieved from SAS on-line). 
	
- Unified interface providing filtered set of Marketing Offers from the both sources (internal BSL structures + SAS).
	
- Registry of blocked (already used) Marketing Offers.
	
- UI for management of client responses to Marketing Offers in SAS.
