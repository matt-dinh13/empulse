## 📝 Notes

A cooling-off period is a period of time following a purchase when the purchaser may choose to cancel a purchase, and return goods which have been supplied, for any reason, and obtain a full refund (consumer rights legislation and practice).
For closed-end loans it is a loan service which allows customer fully repay his/her loan in defined number of days from contract signing. From the system point of view, the service is way of early repayment of the loan without need to pay interests or regular (monthly) fees. On the other hand, the service can be charged a defined fee.
