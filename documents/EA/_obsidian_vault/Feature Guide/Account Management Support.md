# 📁 Account Management Support

> **Type**: Package · **Stereotype**: «feature»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Feature Guide/CLM

## 📝 Notes

Mainly used for displaying of client's account information or credit limit change history, blocking/unblocking account, creation of cardless transaction request for repeated cash disbursement transactions (e.g. Loan on Phone - LOP) on a client's request.

## Details
- Account detail and balances (contains e.g. account number, account type, account status, billing date, due date, limits, etc. 
- Transaction History (displaying transaction history of revolving account)
- Balances (list of account balances (actual and historical)) 
- Unconfirmed Transactions (list of unconfirmed transactions)
- Installment plans (all installment plans related to account)
- Interests (list of interests and their details)
- Installment schedule (REL installment schedule of the contract)
- Block/unblock account(account blocking overview)
- Show credit limit change histor(detail information about credit limit changes of
  particular REL contract)
- Show loyalty account detail (loyalty account detail in LRP)
- Cardless transaction request (creation of repeated cash disbursement (cardless)
  transactions (e.g. loan on phone - LOP) on a client's request
