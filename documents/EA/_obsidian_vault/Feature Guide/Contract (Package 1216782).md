# 📁 Contract

> **Type**: Package · **Stereotype**: «feature»
> **Package**: HomerSelect/BSL/Feature Guide/CLM

## 📝 Notes

The key entity of contract lifecycle management.  It holds the data obtained during the whole contract lifecycle from its sign till finish like: 

- contract statuses, type - REL or CEL, registration status, services applied, sign date, IBAN, references to product, deal, salesroom, and financed commodity, account numbers (for REL contract type)
- It also holds reference to contract documents, DDM (Direct Debit Mandate), Financial parameters of the loan used for installment schedule generation, payment channel.
